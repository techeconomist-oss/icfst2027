About

Where the lab bench meets the investigation

Organised by the Division of Physical Sciences and the Division of Criminology and Forensic Science, Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences.

ICFST-2027 focuses on recent advances in forensic science and technology, from forensic chemistry, physics and biology to digital forensics, toxicology, fingerprint development, questioned documents, forensic psychology and pathology, and the developments still ahead.

Delegates will discuss their research with the aim of building new industry and academic collaborations on the problems forensic science faces today. The conference brings together researchers from academia, industry, practice and investigating agencies.

Rapid Detection of Latent Fingermarks on Porous Surfaces Using Fluorescent Nanopowders
A. Author¹, B. Author²
¹Division of Forensic Science, Example University
Latent fingermarks remain among the most valuable forms of physical evidence at crime scenes, yet their visualisation on porous and multicoloured substrates is often limited by background interference and low contrast. This study reports a simple powder method that improves ridge clarity under ultraviolet illumination while keeping the procedure compatible with subsequent DNA recovery. Results across paper, cardboard and fabric surfaces show third-level detail in most samples.
